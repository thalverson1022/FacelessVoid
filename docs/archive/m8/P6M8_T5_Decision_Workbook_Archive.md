## Accepted inputs and decision policy

- T4's 16 identities, roles and supplied sprite choices are approved. The five existing weapon sprites stay unchanged.
- The user's submitted stats and numeric effects are the starting design inputs, preserved in T4. T4 acceptance does not resolve ambiguous formulas, unspecified chances, acquisition policy or final interaction/balance acceptance. Do not reopen supplied values without explaining a concrete issue.
- Preserve implemented [T3 stat contracts](../../P6M8_T3_Gear_Stat_Implementation_Specification.md): multiplicative per-contribution CDR without an aggregate balance cap, additive Buff Duration, existing All Stacks behavior and inventory economy. New buff families require explicit duration integration.
- Current [combat mechanics](../../Combat_Mechanics_Reference.md) and the [T2 audit](P6M8_T2_Current_Legendary_And_System_Audit.md) establish source behavior; T3 supersedes the audit's old stat pools. Existing engine trigger budgets are diagnostics, not approved gameplay limits for new designs.
- Recommendations below are discussion options only. Record user answers with a decision ID before treating them as accepted rules. Accepted answers are recorded below; unanswered recommendations remain proposals.
- T5 completion requires exact effects, event eligibility/order, relevant formulas, resource use, caps/timers, acquisition assumptions, edge cases and a validation plan, followed by its review checkpoint. T6 prototypes precede broad implementation.

### Round 2 — Poison, Decrepit Blade and Serpent's Coil

- **D017–D018 accepted:** Decrepit Blade’s Poison Strike consumes all actual Poison stacks currently on the enemy and resolves their damage as **one combined Poison hit**, not one packet per stack. The user supplied `base elemental damage × stacks × elemental damage increases`, with one crit roll. Express the pre-defense calculation as `B × N × (1 + E)`, where B is resolved base elemental damage, N is actual Poison stacks consumed, and E is the applicable Increased Elemental Damage total; apply the normal crit damage multiplier if the single crit roll succeeds. Crit chance is a probability, not a direct damage multiplier. No ordinary one/two/three-stack fuel limit. Exact resolved-stat inclusions, mitigation, existing Poison talent multipliers and crit-enabling requirements remain to reconcile; do not silently import or remove them.
- **D019 accepted:** Decrepit replaces Poison Strike’s ordinary single forced Poison tick with the combined all-stack release defined by D017–D018. It forces all current Poison stacks’ damage at once and consumes those stacks; no additional ordinary forced tick is added. Poison Strike retains its normal direct attack damage. Exact same-time natural-tick, subsequent application/cleanse and Consume-hook ordering still needs reconciliation.
- **D020–D021 accepted:** Serpent’s Coil allows both natural Poison ticks and forced Poison activations from Poison Strike to roll Retrigger Chance, including Decrepit’s replacement all-stack release. The retrigger repeats the damage without a second consumption of Poison stacks; it does not require remaining fuel, so Decrepit’s burst can retrigger after consuming all stacks. **D022 accepted:** retriggered Poison can roll Retrigger Chance again and continue the chain, following normal retrigger rules; no Coil-specific one-repeat limit. **D023 accepted:** each Poison retrigger makes its own fresh crit roll; it does not inherit its parent’s crit result. A critical repeat independently rolls Locket’s 20% chance under D016. Precise pre-crit damage snapshot/mitigation semantics and any existing Poison crit-enabling requirement still need reconciliation; do not multiply an inherited critical damage value by another crit multiplier. Existing technical budgets require prototype qualification, not an invented new gameplay cap.
- **D024 accepted:** Poison retriggers also grant Meditation from Helm of the Samurai, extending D014 beyond retriggered skills to include Coil’s repeated Poison damage. Natural or forced original Poison activations do not qualify merely by occurring; this decision concerns their retriggers. Suggested concise wording: “Retriggers grant 1 stack of Meditation.” D016 already allows critical Poison packets to roll Locket independently. This does not classify Poison repeats as attacks or direct-hit crit applications.
- Confirm Wyvern retains doubled natural cadence with the current fuel, suppression and clock rules. Faster ticking does not inherently create more Poison stacks.

**D025 accepted:** all Poison critical damage remains gated by the existing crit-enabling talent, including Decrepit’s combined release and Coil’s repeated damage. D018/D023’s crit rolls apply only when that talent enables Poison crits. Other unresolved Poison timing, modifier and snapshot rules remain on the reconciliation checklist.

### Round 3 — Self buffs, Meditation and Dash

- **D032 accepted:** Crown doubles effects of both active and passive self buffs, explicitly including Dash. It is not limited to skills categorized Self Buff. Enumerate the applicable self-buff families and distinguish enemy debuffs in the final specification; this decision does not classify Penetration/Potency or other enemy-applied statuses as self buffs. Crown cannot normally coexist with Samurai in the helm slot. Which properties are doubled remains the next question.
- **D033 accepted:** Crown doubles self-buff effect strength only. Duration, stack caps and number of stacks granted remain unchanged. Example: Dash grants +10% Increased Attack Speed per stack instead of +5%, with the same 20-stack cap and normal duration scaling. Resolve each buff’s strength representation and stat ordering in the final specification; do not double every numeric field or the whole final player stat.
- **D026 accepted:** Meditation’s stack contributions add: `physical_damage_multiplier = 1 + 0.20 × stacks`, up to five stacks. At five stacks +100% gives one ×2 Physical Damage multiplier; stacks do not compound as `1.2^stacks`. This is a separate multiplier, not +100% added to the ordinary Increased Physical Damage bucket. Timer and stack-grant modifiers remain to specify.
- **D027 accepted for Meditation:** all stacks share one three-second base timer. Every qualifying grant refreshes the full stack group, including a grant at the five-stack cap. Stacks expire together, not individually. D028 confirms Meditation receives existing additive Increased Buff Duration scaling: `3 seconds × (1 + resolved duration increase)` (e.g. +20% gives 3.6 seconds), using T3 timing/rounding conventions. D029 confirms Dash also uses a shared three-second base timer: every grant refreshes all stacks, including at the 20-stack cap, and all stacks expire together. D030 confirms Dash receives existing T3 additive Increased Buff Duration scaling on its shared three-second base timer, just like Meditation (+20% gives 3.6 seconds). Exact expiry ordering and fight cleanup still need specification.
- **D031 accepted:** Wingjammer’s 30% Dash grant chance applies to critical Poison damage packets, including retriggered Poison, as well as critical direct-hit packets. Roll once per critical damage packet, not per Poison stack. Poison crits still require the existing talent under D025. **D063 accepted:** both Meditation and Dash grants receive existing All Stacks bonuses, bounded by their normal caps (five and 20 respectively). Base-one grants with +5 All Stacks request six: Meditation accepts at most five; Dash accepts up to remaining capacity. Crown does not double the grant count under D033. **D064 accepted:** ordinary stack-doubling effects also apply to Meditation and Dash, after All Stacks and before normal caps. Grant order is base one → All Stacks bonus → applicable ordinary doubling → remaining-capacity limit (Meditation five, Dash 20). Shared timer refresh still occurs at cap; Crown’s strength-only rule does not add a second grant multiplier. **D065 accepted:** connected critical packets still roll Locket and Wingjammer if mitigation reduces their damage to zero. Dodged attacks do not qualify. This preserves one roll per eligible critical packet and does not create packets from empty Poison fuel or noncritical Exsanguinate health removal.

### Round 4 — Shred, Exsanguinate and Umbral

- **D034 accepted:** Nightkiss’s +5 Stacks means +5 to the existing All Stacks stat, using its established eligible application, aggregation, cap and doubling rules. It is not a Shred-only bonus or an increase to status capacity. Existing ineligible fixed-charge or fixed-grant sources remain governed by their own contracts; this decision does not broaden All Stacks eligibility.
- **D035 accepted:** Exsanguinate uses the full resolved Armor Shred Value per stack (including Nightkiss and applicable other sources), not actual armor removed from the target. Health removed is `2 × resolved Shred Value × actual Shred stacks consumed`, independent of enemy armor amount.
- **D035 accepted:** Exsanguinate directly removes health, bypassing all enemy mitigation; do not resolve the removal as a mitigated physical or elemental damage packet. **D036 accepted:** health removal is unaffected by player damage bonuses and cannot crit. Its only stated scaling inputs are resolved Shred Value and actual stacks consumed; damage multipliers such as Meditation and Death’s Door do not modify the removal. Crit-dependent Locket/Dash cannot activate from this noncritical removal. Other event-hook semantics, cast interruption and application eligibility remain separate questions.
- **D038 accepted:** Death Pact remains unchanged: consuming Shred with Exsanguinate does not activate it. Do not grant Pact Penetration/Potency from Shred consumption or its consumption-free repeats. Existing Poison/Decay Consume behavior is retained.
- **D037 accepted:** Exsanguinate can retrigger and repeat the original resolved health-removal amount without consuming Shred again or requiring remaining stacks, like the approved Poison repeat behavior. Repeated removal retains D035–D036 mitigation bypass, no player damage modifiers and no crits. The user’s “like poison” direction follows normal retrigger rules, including recursive repeat eligibility; exact event ordering and technical chain qualification remain to reconcile.
- Preserve user-authored Debuff category, 0.3-second cast and eight-second cooldown as inputs; specify minimum time, timing modifiers, interruption, retrigger eligibility, zero Shred and consumption order. Shred Consume does not automatically gain Death Pact behavior.
- **D039 accepted:** Umbral’s +1 Physical Damage per Decay stack adds to Death Strike’s existing damage, including its existing Poison-stack scaling; neither replaces the other. **D040 accepted:** Death Strike leaves Decay stacks intact; its added scaling does not consume them. Exact formula insertion and snapshot timing remain technical specification questions. Preserve existing skill identity and other mechanics unless explicitly revised.
- Gear-granted Death Strike/Poison Strike/Quick Cut: shared stable skill identity, augmentation and unlock deduplication; gear removal must preserve unlocks supplied by another source and prune unavailable macro entries.

### Round 5 — Signet, gold and the other existing weapons

- **D041 accepted:** Signet converts Crit Chance and Crit Damage from all sources, including gear, talents and temporary buffs, rather than gear only. Temporary contributions must participate while active and cease on expiry. **D042 accepted:** use the uncapped Crit Chance total for 2× Attack Speed conversion, before the ordinary effective crit probability cap. Example: 150% Crit Chance converts to +300% Increased Attack Speed. **D043 accepted:** convert only extra Crit Damage above a normal hit, from all sources. If resolved crit multiplier is M (normal hit = 1), the Increased Physical Damage contribution is `2 × (M - 1)`. Example: ×2.5 crit damage has +150% above a normal hit and converts to +300% Increased Physical Damage. The ordinary-hit 100% component is not converted; inherent extra crit damage and all other sources are included. No timing/snapshot exception is inferred.
- **D044 accepted:** Signet prevents all crits, including Poison crits even with the existing crit-enabling talent. Crit-dependent effects such as Locket recovery, Dash grants and crit-to-status applications cannot activate while Signet is equipped. This suppression does not erase conversion inputs under D041–D043. Resolve All Stats and derived conversions once without cycles; RNG handling remains technical reconciliation.
- **D045 accepted:** Bandit grants whole +1 Base Physical Damage increments per complete ten stash gold: `bonus = min(100, floor(stash_gold / 10))` for nonnegative stash gold. Examples: 0–9 gold gives +0, 10–19 gives +1, 999 gives +99, and 1,000 or more gives the +100 cap. This replaces the current fractional +0.1-per-gold signature behavior when implemented. **D046 accepted:** recalculate this bonus live whenever stash gold changes during combat, including theft; do not keep a fight-start snapshot. Gains/losses that cross ten-gold thresholds change the bonus, subject to the +100 cap. Exact event ordering and cap placement relative to other modifiers remain technical reconciliation. The supplied +50% Gold remains the input.
- **D047 accepted:** Bejeweled has a 40% chance to use the skill’s minimum cast time without bypassing cooldowns, retaining that existing lightning-fast behavior. It changes execution timing rather than granting another cast. Preserve existing scheduler/resource/failure rules unless separately revised; exact category coverage and RNG placement remain technical reconciliation against the existing path.
- **D048 accepted:** +15 Base Damage on Lolzorz’s Locket and Wingjammer means +15 Base Physical Damage. Locket’s separate +15 Base Elemental Damage remains unchanged. Gold/Gold Rewards mapping, new weapon base ranges/families and fixed-affix scaling/All Stats treatment still need reconciliation.

### Round 6 — Death's Door and the special Coin

- **D049 historical intent; D050 current-phase decision:** retain the existing per-encounter attempt system for now. Death’s Door uses attempts lost in the current encounter; a loss grants the bonus on the retry, and a new encounter resets the count. The user explicitly accepts the incentive to lose the first attempt for the retry bonus. A run-wide lives system and future Alchemist/account extra lives are deferred to a later phase; do not implement a run-wide pool for M8.
- **D050 accepted multiplier:** `max(1, 2 × missing_attempts)`; 0/1/2/3/4 missing → ×1/×2/×4/×6/×8. Applies to all damage, explicitly including Poison, but excludes Exsanguinate’s direct health removal. Later-phase life-system changes can reuse this schedule. Existing run-ending/terminal rules still apply; no attack is granted after run end. **D066 accepted:** Death’s Door gives no signature damage bonus in the opening unlimited-retry encounter, regardless of its recorded failures; use ×1 there. User notes the item is not normally obtainable for that first fight. This is a boundary rule, not a request for a new acquisition route. Exact sampling/update order and Practice configuration still need technical reconciliation.
- **D051 accepted:** Mysterious Lucky Coin doubles total Magic Find: `effective_magic_find = 2 × total_magic_find`. User example: 150% becomes 300%. This is not +100 percentage points or ×100; zero total remains zero. Apply the signature once to the resolved total, rather than doubling each source and then the total again. Final presentation can say “Double your total Magic Find.”
- **D052 accepted:** destroying the ordinary Lucky Coin unlocks Mysterious Lucky Coin’s appearance by adding it to eligible pools; it does not immediately grant or transform into the Legendary. After unlocking it can be found in the shop or enemy loot drops. Before unlocking, those sources must exclude it. **D053 accepted:** unlock is current-run only and resets for a new run. Achievements and account-wide persistence remain future-phase work. **D054 accepted:** destroying the ordinary Lucky Coin (`gear.lucky_coin`) unlocks the Legendary whether destroyed from inventory or while equipped. Selling or unequipping is not destruction and does not qualify under this requirement. Repeated unlocks, exact pool weights/drop integration, Practice access and within-run save/resume policy remain to specify; run-local scope does not mean session-only. T2’s current baseline has ordinary Legendary acquisition primarily through contract shops; the requested enemy-loot availability must be explicitly integrated, not claimed already supported.

### Round 7 — Equipment, acquisition assumptions and prototype handoff

- **D056 accepted:** players may equip one Legendary in each existing gear slot simultaneously, with no extra global Legendary limit. Standard one-item-per-slot rules still apply; chests use Armor, so up to five Legendaries can coexist. Preserve existing stable IDs and settle display-name spellings separately.
- **D055 accepted:** all ordinary Legendaries are eligible for both enemy loot drops and shops. Mysterious Lucky Coin joins those same eligible pools only after its current-run unlock. Actual rates, relative item weights, slot selection, owned-ID filtering, rewards, duplicates and overflow replacement remain to specify. Enemy-loot Legendary integration is required against the current shop-oriented baseline; eligibility alone does not adopt a new rate or broad loot-system rewrite.
- **D057 accepted:** exclude already-owned Legendary identities from shops and enemy loot, counting both equipped and inventory copies. Selling or destroying removes that ownership exclusion and makes the identity eligible again, subject to any independent unlock gate (Mysterious Lucky Coin still needs D053’s current-run unlock). Outstanding-offer snapshots, concurrent selections and all-owned fallback behavior remain technical/acquisition reconciliation.
- **D060 accepted:** after an acquisition outcome selects Legendary rarity, choose uniformly among eligible Legendary identities, rather than uniformly among equipment slots. Mysterious Lucky Coin has the same item weight once unlocked; owned-item exclusions remain under D057. This sets conditional item selection, not the chance of rolling Legendary rarity in the first place. **D061 accepted:** retain current depth-based shop Legendary rarity chances unchanged for now. This includes preserving the current shop rarity-roll policy rather than introducing a roster-size-based increase. **D062 accepted:** preserve existing enemy-loot roll rules and rarity behavior; do not introduce shop-matched enemy-loot odds, additional rolls or gear drops. Broader roll-system and loot-rarity review is deferred. D055/D060 define eligible Legendary identity selection only where the retained acquisition rules permit a Legendary outcome. Any gap between requested source eligibility and the existing outcome paths must be documented, not solved by inventing new rolls. D061 does not create new Tavern availability or alter unrelated shop rules.
- **D058 accepted:** Legendary authored stats stay fixed at the user-supplied values regardless of contract depth; do not apply generated gear depth scaling to those affixes. Normal build bonuses still apply under their existing eligibility rules. This does not override item-specific caps, Exsanguinate’s no-damage-bonus contract or Crown’s strength-only rules. **D059 accepted:** Decrepit Blade and Nightkiss Kitana use the existing Legendary weapon base damage range of 21–27, separate from listed flat-damage affixes. Weapon-family metadata and any remaining modifier ordering still need specification. No item XP/upgrades or new slots are inferred.

D038, 2026-10-15. This is a formatting reference of names, source IDs and aliases, without mechanical definitions.

Whole phrases match case-insensitively with Unicode word boundaries, longest first. Named Buff references use the existing Self Buff cyan; stats, statuses, skill references and ordinary prose remain cream. Separate talent values retain gold. Skill headers and unlock references retain their ordinary styling; an overlapping Buff name is cream in an Unlocks row.

Crit Chance and Crit Damage are the canonical short forms. Their Critical Chance/Critical Damage forms are accepted aliases. Distinct phrases such as Physical Damage and Increased Physical Damage retain their own words. Ordinary gold, stack, attack, hit, damage, steal and hold prose is not globally capitalized. Resource names participate in longest-name matching to prevent partial collisions such as Precision Attacks versus Precision.

Matching priority resolves a shared spelling to its explicit Buff/status entry before resource-name duplicates. The table consolidates source IDs sharing that spelling.

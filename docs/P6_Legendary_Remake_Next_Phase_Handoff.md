# Legendary Item remake — next-phase handoff

> Current roadmap update (2026-10-17, [P6M8-D001](P6M8_Legendary_Item_Revamp_And_Expansion_Tracker.md#decision-log)): Legendary Item Revamp and Expansion is now Phase 6 M8. Former M8 integrated playtest/refinement is M9; former M9 regression/closeout is M10. The historical record below retains its original numbering and deferrals; the next-phase Legendary deferral is superseded. M9 owns inherited fixture/signature reconciliation; M10 owns broad regression and engine/export qualification. No item design or experimental Bandit curve is adopted by this update.

2026-10-08. User direction: “We are going to do a separate Legendary Item remake in the next phase.”

Legendary-specific refinement is deferred from M6-T10 to that remake. Keep the current live items. The Bandit Blade diminishing-return patch is an unadopted experiment, not an approved design or a prerequisite for closing M6. Its numerical approval and paired-candidate playtest request are withdrawn from T10.

Carry forward the T9/T10 evidence:

- Bandit Blade's unbounded stash-gold scaling can overwhelm encounter requirements and reduce the cost of simplifying a sequence.
- Flat coefficient cuts also weaken early item usefulness. The tested diminishing-return curve preserved more early progression, but none of the tested candidate full journeys completed the entire catalog; it is not an accepted balance solution.
- Evaluate remade Legendaries through earned full runs as well as matched snapshots. Compare coherent/informed preparation and costly mistakes, item acquisition timing, spending versus hoarding, and counter matchups.
- Preserve the user's hard-game direction: optimization and synergy should matter, weak choices may fail, and severe build counters plus informed tag-based avoidance are intentional.
- Revisit full-run difficulty after the remake. Existing M6 results describe the current item system and cannot establish balance of future items. Do not compensate enemies now to offset a remake whose design is not yet defined.

Evidence: `project/reports/p6_m6_t9` and `project/reports/p6_m6_t10`; investigation: `docs/P6M6_T10_Balance_Refinement_And_Acceptance_Review.md`. All raw evidence, isolated experiments and proposed patches remain retained as reference. No numerical curve, cap, item roster or implementation plan is prescribed for the remake by this handoff.

Under P6M6-D015, the user accepted the retained M6 scope and evidence and closed T10. Further hands-on balance review is assigned to the user’s substantial playtesting during next milestone’s icon/UI feedback. Legendary balance remains a deferred exception for this remake. No Legendary-specific approval is pending; no experimental Bandit curve is adopted. T11 and M6 are now Complete; see [final closeout and M7 handoff](P6M6_T11_Closeout_And_M7_Handoff.md).

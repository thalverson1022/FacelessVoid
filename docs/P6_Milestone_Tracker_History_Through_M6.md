## Goal and Scope

Redesign Assassin, Bladedancer, Shadow, and Thief with distinct identities,
meaningful progression, gear synergies, and readable combat behavior.

Phase 6 includes the supporting build model, shared combat mechanics, talent and
skill UI, temporary-effect presentation, icons, Practice Room, Balance Lab,
save compatibility, and enemy/progression rebalance needed for the new trees.
Enemy tuning is explicitly in scope; its magnitude and method remain undecided.

Preserve the audited Phase 5 gear and generated-contract foundation. New classes,
a gear-system rewrite, broad route or economy redesign, and wholesale combat
resolver replacement remain outside this scope. Record any later scope change
in the decision log before incorporating it into the roadmap.

Baseline: the `phase-6-skill-tree-redesign` branch incorporates Enigma audit
commit `d0e025ae9ff6b86aa389582443be6382547cb4cc`. See the
[integration notes](P6_Audited_Baseline_Integration.md) for historical validation
and remaining limitations. Those results do not validate future Phase 6 changes.

# P6M8-T3 — Gear stat implementation and verification

2026-10-22. **T3 Complete under P6M8-D008.** The user requested implementation and verification of the [approved specification](P6M8_T3_Gear_Stat_Implementation_Specification.md). The replacement pool and five new stat identities are implemented. M8 remains in progress; T4 roster and identity design is next. This closeout does not approve broader balance or a Legendary roster.

## Delivered behavior

- New generation uses the exact 99 positive entries and 33 Basic-derived drawbacks, with approved weights, ranges, rarity/depth scaling, ordering and uniqueness/Chaos rules. Removed pool entries remain valid on old saved gear. Fixed Legendary resources, signatures and values are unchanged.
- Cooldown reduction multiplies individual retained factors in displayed slot order, with All Stats applied to each affix first. The scheduler uses the unrounded factor; positive cooldowns have a 1ms floor and authored zero stays zero. The accepted 99.7536% endpoint resolves a 10s cooldown to 25ms. Near-zero cooldowns remain subject to windup, resources, macro order and existing trigger budgets.
- Buff Duration adds without a balance cap. Footwork, Bypass, Dance of Death, Master of Poisons, Touch of Darkness, Beguiling Presence, Penetration and Potency use checked scaled timers. Death Pact extends the defense-debuff base before duration multiplication. Refresh, caps, consumption, independent instances and untimed charges retain their rules.
- Critical direct contributions independently try Penetration then Potency after existing hit applications and before finalize/cleanse/children. Zero chance consumes no extra RNG; positive chance, including 100%, consumes one draw per stat. Ordinary base-one grants receive bonus stacks and doubling. Connected zero-damage crits qualify; Poison tick crits do not.
- An equipped Inventory Economy Special imports only signed Gold, Discount and Magic Find from carried ownership occurrences. It applies combined floors/All Stats/derivations once. Identical cached resources can represent separate copies. Equip/swap/unequip move occurrences correctly; pending offers cannot contribute or discount themselves.
- Practice supports up to three carried items, slot/rarity/affix editing and removal, precise raw inputs, legacy removed-affix editing and read-only fixed Legendaries. Shared stats show timing/proc values; the scrollable source inspector exposes raw/effective CDR factors and carried sources. Equipment comparisons recompute ownership and derived outputs.
- Adventure writes version 3 and reads 2/3; Practice writes version 2 and reads 1/2. Old Practice defaults to empty carried gear. Loads validate detached candidates and do not rewrite supported old files. Invalid new modifiers, unknown explicit IDs, malformed carried records and active timer overflow fail without replacing state/files. Existing rolls, metadata, pending offers and authored reload behavior are preserved. No tree revision or enum reorder was introduced.
- Finite timer arithmetic and non-finite damage/state failures produce invalid simulations with diagnostics. Large finite direct damage retains floating-point rounding rather than overflowing an integer conversion. These arithmetic protections introduce no gameplay balance cap.

## Retained failures and balance handoff

Four original suites (`p6_status_accounting_test`, `p6_status_interactions_test`, `p6_poison_contract_test`, `p6_gold_contract_test`) still fail the six pre-T2 signature expectations for seeds 1, 103, 11, 23, 42 and 7. These are the inherited compatibility expectations documented in the [M7 exception register](P6M7_T11_Closeout_And_M8_Handoff.md#bounded-repairs-and-exception-triage); no replacement goldens were written to hide them. They are excluded from the 33 passes.

An additional exploratory run of `p6_m6_adventure_integration_test` fails at its obsolete `Contract secondary` step: it requests the secondary when accepting the first contract. Retained D024 earns that choice after completing the first contract. The current secondary-reward and autosave suites pass. This old flow fixture is not counted as a pass and was not rewritten as part of T3.

Passing runs can still emit the inherited certificate-store and ObjectDB/resource-at-exit diagnostics. General environment, clean-teardown and export qualification remains M10 work. Broad earned-run playtesting remains M9 work; T3 does not claim export or launch qualification.

The synthetic economy trajectory reproduces a concrete **39-gold purchase/resale profit**: two carried Chaos discount rolls of 81% make an 80-gold Legendary cost 1 and sell for 40. The subsequent reroll costs 5, and the next costs 10; selling discount gear removes future contribution, full inventory blocks acquisition without payment, and pending offer rolls stay fixed. This witness establishes individual arbitrage, not an unbounded loop. Price/resale/discount/talent coefficients remain as approved. T10 must review this surface alongside sustained buffs, maintained debuffs, near-zero cooldowns and theft/derived-stat interactions before broader balance acceptance.

No additional core T3 implementation choice remains open. T4 can now design the Legendary roster against this implemented gear/stat baseline.

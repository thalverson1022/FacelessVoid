## Goal and scope

Review the existing Legendary items, define and implement a coherent revamp and a set of new Legendary items, add a few reviewed stats to the regular gear pool, and support only the acquisition, progression, persistence, presentation, combat feedback and validation systems required by those items. Keep the game hard: optimization, synergy, preparation and informed matchup avoidance should matter; weak choices may fail, and severe counters may be intentional.

This milestone does not prescribe a roster count, item values, slot changes or an acquisition policy. Those remain design questions for T2–T5. New gear stat identities, values and pool rules are resolved in T3 before implementation. It does not authorize a broad gear or economy rewrite, new classes, endless progression, a general route redesign, or enemy compensation for a not-yet-defined item system. Existing Bandit Blade diminishing-return work is an unadopted experiment. Prior M6 full-run results describe the old item system and are not proof of future item balance.

Use isolated saves for validation. The [minimum T7 save integration policy](P6M8_T7_Save_Integration_Policy.md) was recorded before the run-local Coin flag/version-4 edits; T8 broader acquisition/persistence qualification is accepted under D082; retain the documented compatibility and durability limits. Preserve the accepted M7 artwork and player-feedback conventions unless a concrete item-specific need is reviewed.

Start from the [M7 closeout and handoff](P6M7_T11_Closeout_And_M8_Handoff.md), [M7 tracker](P6M7_Icons_And_Player_Feedback_Completion_Tracker.md), [M6 closeout and M7 handoff](P6M6_T11_Closeout_And_M7_Handoff.md), and [Legendary remake handoff](P6_Legendary_Remake_Next_Phase_Handoff.md). M9 owns broader earned full-run and unusual-build playtesting/refinement. M10 owns broad regression, exports and teardown qualification.

## T8 execution steps

T8 execution and findings are accepted under D082; T8 is Complete; [report](P6M8_T8_Acquisition_Progression_And_Persistence_Verification.md). The following sequence was carried out using accepted T7 integration. [T7 evidence](P6M8_T7_Implementation_And_Verification.md) and [minimum save policy](P6M8_T7_Save_Integration_Policy.md) are the baseline.

1. **Audit acquisition paths and remaining gaps.** Trace shop generation/rerolls/purchases, explicit authored Legendary rewards, pending offers and inventory/equipment transitions. Preserve current depth-based shop odds, equal eligible-ID weights, prices/capacity/fallback rules and generated-enemy loot outcomes. D068 keeps new generated-enemy Legendary outcomes deferred.
2. **Qualify eligibility through actual transactions.** Prove all eligible roster IDs can appear; equipped and inventory-owned IDs are excluded; selling/destroying restores eligibility; locked Coin stays excluded. Test all-owned Cursed fallback and stale offer/reward rejection without payment or reward loss.
3. **Complete Coin lifecycle coverage.** Exercise both destruction routes, no immediate grant, no unlock from sale/unequip, repeated destruction, same-run reload, new-run reset and Practice isolation. Test transitions with pending offers/rewards, not just isolated flag helpers.
4. **Finish progression/equipment integration.** Obtain, purchase/claim, equip/swap/unequip and sell/destroy items across all five slots. Preserve shared skill ownership and macro pruning, fixed affixes/base ranges across depth and build bonuses. Verify saved Death’s Door retry context, opening unlimited retries, new encounters and terminal-state restrictions. Keep global lives unchanged.
5. **Broaden persistence compatibility checks.** Extend the documented version4 policy before any further format changes. Use copied supported-version2/3/4 fixtures across inventory, equipment, shops and pending rewards; retain ordinary/T3 gear values and unrelated run state. Check missing/malformed unlock flags, authored IDs, failed/future loads without mutation, previous-reader rejection, and Practice save/load isolation including its attempt input. Preserve real and unrelated saves; no account persistence or power-loss guarantee is implied.
6. **Verify and report for review.** Use fresh disposable projects/isolated saves, transaction-to-save/resume journeys and focused regressions. Fix scope-relevant gaps, render any changed acquisition UI, and record evidence, inherited failures and T9/T10 handoffs before requesting T8 review.

## T9 execution steps

T9 implementation and evidence are ready for user review under D084; [report](P6M8_T9_Presentation_And_Combat_Feedback_Verification.md). Execution started under D083 from the accepted [T7 implementation](P6M8_T7_Implementation_And_Verification.md), [T8 qualification](P6M8_T8_Acquisition_Progression_And_Persistence_Verification.md), approved T5 mechanics and M7 presentation conventions. Existing icons, selector controls, buff/cooldown replay and health-removal transport are implemented; audit and finish their player-facing coverage rather than reimplementing them.

1. **Audit all16 items across actual surfaces.** Inventory/equipment, shops, rewards, comparison cards, character stats, skill unlocks, Practice selectors and combat logs must use the correct identity, slot, supplied icon and current fixed values. Preserve the approved spellings and artwork.
2. **Finish accurate, readable descriptions.** State meaningful conditions, caps, durations, conversions and exclusions without exposing implementation internals. Explain Mithril's local chance, Bandit's live cap, Crown's bonus-strength rule, Signet's no-crit conversion, Death's Door encounter attempts and the Coin's run-local unlock as appropriate to the surface. Cross-check wording against live mechanics and the approved spec.
3. **Verify comparisons and build feedback.** Show effective stats, skill gains/losses and conditional signature effects accurately when buying, equipping or replacing an item. Keep fixed affixes read-only, avoid misleading numeric treatment of special effects, and check full-inventory/owned/locked action feedback and Practice restoration.
4. **Complete skill/buff/combat feedback.** Inspect Exsanguinate's Shred consumption, armor restoration and distinct health removal in live HP, popups, logs and recap. Verify Meditation/Dash stacks, strength, refresh and expiry, Crown-adjusted magnitudes, Locket cooldown recovery and Poison Consume/retrigger distinctions. Keep initial procs, repeated skills and natural Poison understandable without flooding the display.
5. **Render representative actual-size cases using isolated saves.** Cover all five slots and all16 item tooltips/cards, long descriptions/comparisons, Exsanguinate, Crown buffs, no-crit Signet and dense legal retrigger chains. Inspect clipping, icon legibility, timing, hover/focus, playback speed/skip and existing reduced-motion/accessibility behavior. Preserve M7 conventions and report limitations.
6. **Run focused presentation regressions and return evidence for review.** Check displayed values/text against authoritative events and resolved stats, confirm UI edits do not alter gameplay or loot rules, fix discrepancies, and record screenshots and remaining T10 balance/exploit or T11 comprehension questions. Final T9 presentation acceptance remains with the user.

## Tracking and validation rules

- Use `Not started`, `In progress`, `Pending input`, `Blocked`, `Complete` or `Deferred`; explain blockers/deferrals and name the next action or owner.
- Record concrete design approvals and evidence in this tracker or linked task documents. T1 approval is planning approval only.
- Keep detailed inventories, specifications and evidence in linked task documents as they are produced; do not claim future playtesting from automated checks or old M6 results.
- Use isolated saves and protect real player saves. Establish stat compatibility policy in T3 and item compatibility policy in T8 before changing persisted data.
- Preserve the accepted M7 visuals and feedback baseline unless an item-specific change is reviewed.
- Preserve hard difficulty and intentional counterplay. Distinguish item defects, exploit paths and presentation issues from intended counters.
- Pure documentation edits require local link and status/content checks, not gameplay suites.

#### Discount/resale profitability

Current [BuildState](../project/scripts/autoload/build_state.gd) uses `max(1, floor(base_price * max(0, 1 - discount)))` for purchases and `max(1, floor(base_price * 0.5))` for ordinary resale. Sale does not consult price paid. `buy_shop_offer()` consumes the offer, requires an inventory opening and charges once; selling requires the actual owned occurrence and removes it. Inventory capacity is three, but selling frees space for subsequent purchases, so capacity does not limit a shop to three total trades. Every batch has six offers; each paid reroll replaces the batch and costs 5g, 10g, 15g, and so on. Reroll cost resets on the next shop round.

The existing [21-check transaction diagnostic and raw witnesses](../project/reports/m9_t10_risk_review_fixed/m9_t10_risk_review/m9_t10_risk/evidence.json) show actual one-time purchase/sale accounting: Golden Greeves alone gives 40% discount and a Basic trade loses 1g (10g buy / 9g sell); with a generated 23% Epic discount weapon, the trade earns 3g (6g / 9g); a generated 54% Chaos weapon alone earns 1g (8g / 9g). These deliberately assembled legal items/bank are not an earned-run incidence sample. The [T10 earned review](P6M9_T10_Focused_Progression_Review.md#discountresale-risk) reports 60 profitable quoted offers in historical five-slot physical play, no observed profitable quotes in six current paths, and no deliberate realized arbitrage by its policy. Absence in that small current sample does not remove the legal avenue.

Additional static bound: [current tier prices](../project/scripts/systems/gear_generator.gd) top out at 80g, so resale is at most 40g and any purchased item's trade margin is at most 39g. Even a generously assumed batch of six maximum-margin trades yields at most 234g. The 47th reroll costs 235g and later rerolls cost more. Thus within one unchanged shop, repeated buy/sell trading alone cannot fund an indefinitely self-sustaining sequence of rerolls under these rules. This is an upper-bound argument, not a claim that such a batch is obtainable, that practical farming is insignificant, or that profit across later shops is capped at 234g.

**Recommendation: accept current resale profitability for M10**, retaining economy-build rewards and the accepted tuning. Evidence establishes profitable trades, but does not establish a duplicate-sale bug or a self-funding infinite reroll loop. Practical profit and gold-driven combat power can still be large; their prevalence remains unmeasured. Acceptance is a design choice, not a balance-safety certificate.

If the user instead wants to prohibit profit, the concrete alternative is resale capped by acquisition price for bought occurrences while retaining normal resale for earned gear. This would require paid-cost provenance, occurrence ownership, save/load rules and a decision for already owned items. [GearItem](../project/scripts/resources/gear_item.gd) currently has no paid-cost field, and [SaveSystem](../project/scripts/systems/save_system.gd) represents authored resources by path. It is not a one-line sale-price fix. A 50% discount cap is another option, but would directly weaken high-discount builds. Neither change is adopted or scheduled.

#### Extreme trigger chains

The [T9 fixture](../project/tests/fixtures/m9_t9_mechanics_fixture.gd) constructs legal scale-1/depth-12 endpoint affixes, rather than seeded earned drops. Its [budget test](../project/tests/m9_t9_combined_mechanics_test.gd) combines capped ordinary retrigger chance, Critical Poison, Coil, Scales and Lightning, but directly derives stats using a talent carrier and creates diagnostic seeding/Poison Strike skills outside a complete ranked Adventure build. The [existing witness](../project/reports/m9_t9_mechanics/m9_t9_combined_mechanics_test/m9_t9_mechanics.json) records 928 candidates at seed 1 and exhaustion at 1,024 for seed 7. Legal gear membership is established; ordinary earned-build frequency and an authored-build reproduction are not. Authored Poison Strike also has forced-tick behavior through [Poison contracts](../project/scripts/systems/p6_poison_contracts.gd), so diagnostic construction does not prove ordinary builds cannot reach the limit.

In [CombatResolver](../project/scripts/systems/combat_resolver.gd), 1,024 is a per-root candidate-evaluation budget, not a whole-fight cast/damage total. Candidates can consume it before eligibility/chance filtering. Exceeding it invalidates the simulation. The separate depth-16 guard stops further descendants; it does not establish global headroom or have the same failure behavior as budget exhaustion. The [T10 proc-budget review](P6M9_T10_Focused_Progression_Review.md#proc-budget-risk) observed current maxima of 10 candidates and depth 4 in its bounded earned sample; those are observations, not safe maxima.

Player consequence: [Adventure fight handling](../project/scenes/combat/combat_screen.gd) detects invalid results before theft credit, result/history recording, normal fight completion and result autosave. It exposes the invalid diagnostic/log and calls `cancel_invalid_fight()`, returning to preparation without consuming a loss attempt. BuildState also rejects invalid results from DPS/history and Overkill. This protects accounting but does not make the build usable: unchanged build/encounter/retry inputs preserve the deterministic seed, so pressing Fight again can reproduce failure. Affected players may need to change preparation. Existing evidence does not establish a current player occurrence, universal recovery or crash/power-loss durability.

**Recommendation: explicitly defer broader authored/earned-build trigger usability investigation for this bounded milestone**, retaining the 1,024/depth-16 guards and fail-closed behavior. No demonstrated ordinary-build failure presently justifies raising limits, truncating outcomes into a win/loss or changing combat. Retain the risk at M11 handoff as an acknowledged limitation, not an automatic implementation/test campaign. A concrete authored-build/player reproduction should reopen it with build, rotation, enemy, seed and failure details; any resulting check/correction would need a bounded agreed scope.
